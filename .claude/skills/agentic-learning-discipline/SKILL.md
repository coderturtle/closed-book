# Understanding check

The real exam this workshop prepares you for forbids the agent you used to build the hands-on
tier. The deterministic checker doesn't care how an artifact got written, and it never will -
"the checker passed" and "I understand why" are different claims, and the closed-book checkpoint
is where that gap stops being hideable.

Before opening this module's closed-book checkpoint, close the artifact you just built and,
without looking back at it, write two or three sentences: what did you actually configure, and
why does that make it correct rather than merely working.

If that's uncomfortable to do, that's worth noticing on its own - it usually means the hands-on
tier was delegated rather than understood, and a slower second pass is worth more than the
checkpoint catching it for you. Not scored, not something Coachgremlin checks. It's for your own
use.
